9.1.1 List the Members of the Solar System

Our Solar System is a huge cosmic family! Let's explore The Center of the Solar System, All Members of the Solar System, and the 8 Planets in Sequence from the Sun.

PART 1

The Center of the Solar System

☀️ Center / Pusat

The Sun (Matahari)

A giant self-luminous star

🔥 1. Position in Space

The Sun is located right at the center of the Solar System. All other celestial members orbit around the Sun in fixed elliptical paths called orbits.

💡 2. Primary Source of Light & Heat

The Sun is the only member that emits its own light and heat. The planets and moons do not produce light; they only reflect sunlight back into space.

🧲 3. Strong Gravitational Pull

Because the Sun is colossal and massive, its huge gravitational attraction keeps all planets, asteroids, meteoroids, and comets safely travelling along their orbits.
